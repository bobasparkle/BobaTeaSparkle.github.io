Chapter 232 — Part 1

At that moment, Zhao Yue pushed open the door and entered. Smiling slightly, she said, Miss, it’s time for dinner.

Li Weiyang glanced at her and immediately understood that Zhao Yue had come in because she was worried about leaving her alone with Prince Jing, Yuan Ying. She could not help but shake her head with a quiet laugh. This girl was becoming bolder and bolder.

Li Weiyang did not expose her thoughts. She simply smiled and asked, “Your Highness, would you like to stay and dine with us?”

Yuan Ying knew that the Guo family always ate together and that Li Weiyang’s invitation was merely polite courtesy. Nevertheless, he smiled faintly and replied, “In that case, I shall respectfully accept your invitation. Jia’er, please go ahead.”

Li Weiyang smiled warmly and walked out first. Prince Jing followed behind her, glancing at Zhao Yue with a half-smile. In his eyes, the girl beside Li Weiyang was quite unusual. Not only was she highly skilled in martial arts, but she also always seemed to prevent him from being alone with Li Weiyang, which suggested that she had another identity or background. Recalling that Yuan Lie also had a guard named Zhao Nan, Yuan Ying immediately understood the situation.

The two walked to the main hall, where bursts of laughter and lively conversation drifted out from inside.

Princess Chenliu spotted Li Weiyang and Yuan Ying at once and said with a smile, “You two have finally arrived! Come, sit down together.”

Prince Jing smiled and strode forward, taking the seat prepared for him. “Grandmother, has something good happened? Why are you in such high spirits?”

Princess Chenliu smiled broadly, her eyes nearly disappearing with laughter. “His Majesty is selecting a husband for Princess Shouchun and has chosen outstanding young men from the noble families to participate. Our family already has two suitable candidates, does it not?” As she spoke, her gaze lingered on Guo Dun and Guo Dao.

Guo Dun looked up at the sky as though he had heard nothing. Guo Dao merely smiled faintly and remained silent. No one answered Princess Chenliu, making the atmosphere somewhat awkward.

Princess Ali glared at Guo Dun, then lowered her head and began fiddling with the chopsticks in her hand. Li Weiyang could not help but laugh.

Princess Chenliu also sensed that something was wrong. “Why are you both acting so strangely? I have met Princess Shouchun before. She is beautiful and gentle, unlike Empress Pei’s spoiled daughters. This time, His Majesty took pity on her and decided to personally select a good husband for her. He ordered every major family to recommend someone to participate. One of you must go eventually. Guo Dun, since you are the elder brother, you should participate this time.”

The moment he heard this, Guo Dun sprang to his feet and waved his hands repeatedly. “Grandmother, please stop teasing me. How could the princess possibly take a liking to someone as careless as me? I would only bring shame upon the Guo family.”

Princess Chenliu was startled. “How could that be? Aren’t you skilled in martial arts? There will also be a martial arts competition this time. If you defeat the others, you will naturally become the princess’s husband.”

Guo Dun glanced at Princess Ali, who sat quietly to the side with her head lowered. A faint blush had appeared on her cheeks.

Li Weiyang looked at the two of them and smiled slightly. “Grandmother, I’m afraid this matter is not so simple.”

Princess Chenliu looked at Li Weiyang in surprise. “Jia’er, why do you say that? Do you not have confidence in your Fourth Brother?”

Li Weiyang gently shook her head. “It is not that I lack confidence in Fourth Brother. However, the selection process for the imperial son-in-law begins with a written examination. Only those who pass may proceed to the martial arts test. Fourth Brother has never enjoyed studying, not even as a child. If he fails the written examination, things will not go well.”

Everyone immediately understood.

Madam Guo had already noticed Guo Dun’s feelings for Princess Ali. She was very fond of the innocent and cheerful princess and did not wish to oppose her son’s feelings. “Since His Majesty has ordered every family to recommend one person, then...” Her gaze shifted toward her youngest son, Guo Dao.

Ordinarily, Guo Dao would have shown great interest in such an event, but now he appeared unusually indifferent. Guo Cheng coughed lightly, and Guo Dao suddenly came back to himself. He glanced at Princess Chenliu, then at Madam Guo. Finally, his gaze settled on Li Weiyang. His handsome eyes carried a faint smile, as though he were hiding other thoughts.

Seeing this, Li Weiyang was slightly startled. She recalled Guo Dao’s strange behavior in the tent that day, and an odd feeling briefly crossed her mind.

At that moment, Prince Jing Yuan Ying smiled faintly and said, “In my opinion, Guo Dao is the most suitable candidate. He excels in both literature and martial arts and is also eloquent. Even if he does not win, he will not disgrace the Guo family. Moreover, this is a good opportunity to observe the hidden strengths of the major families.”

As expected of Prince Jing, his observations were always sharp and perceptive.

Duke Qi nodded. “They certainly will not miss such a grand occasion. First, marrying Princess Shouchun would be an immense honor. Second...” He did not finish the sentence, but Li Weiyang understood immediately. Using the competition to display the younger generation’s talents would help secure the clan’s future and intimidate rival families. Even if they did not reveal their full strength, they would still expose part of their abilities and gain insight into others.

Li Weiyang thought for a moment, and a faint smile appeared on her usually calm face.

After speaking, Duke Qi looked at Guo Dao and said, “Since everyone believes you are suitable, then go. Just do not bring shame upon the Guo family. There is no need for you to win at all costs.”

In other words, he was not being forced to succeed. Guo Dao nodded casually upon hearing this. After all, he had only agreed to participate, not necessarily to marry the princess. If he lost, no one could blame him. Thinking this, he smiled slightly.

Guo Cheng looked at him, his expression complicated. Of everyone present, he alone truly understood what his younger brother was thinking. That was why he remained silent while the others spoke.

Princess Chenliu greatly enjoyed the meal. She admired Princess Shouchun very much and hoped that her grandson would distinguish himself among the great noble families. Seeing how pleased she was, Li Weiyang said nothing further.

After lunch, Guo Dao was the first to leave the hall.

Guo Cheng followed after him and stopped him in the corridor. “Fifth Brother.”

Guo Dao stopped and turned around when he saw who it was. “Third Brother, do you need something?”

Guo Cheng sighed. “About this year’s imperial son-in-law selection...” Before he could finish speaking, Guo Dao interrupted him.

“Didn’t I already promise to participate? What are you worried about?”

Of course Guo Cheng was worried. After a moment of silence, he said, “I know you do not want to marry Princess Shouchun.”

Guo Dao smiled, but there was a trace of melancholy in his expression. “Since you already know, why ask? If it were you, would you abandon Cousin Han Lin to marry a princess you have never even met?”

Guo Cheng fell silent for a moment before replying, “We have met Princess Shouchun before. Do you remember? When we were children, we entered the palace to visit Consort Hui and met her there.”

Guo Dao merely smiled faintly and replied coldly, “Even if she were as beautiful as a celestial maiden and as gentle as water, what would it matter if she is not the person I like?”

For a long time, Guo Cheng could not respond.

At first, he had pursued Han Lin only to reject the Wen family’s daughter. But over time, he had come to discover Han Lin’s quiet virtues. She was gentle, reserved, and wholeheartedly devoted to him. In her presence, he always felt a sense of peace and satisfaction. Gradually, his feelings had changed. This was what people called affection growing over time.

If he were forced to abandon Han Lin in pursuit of a princess, he would find it impossible. Because of this, he could understand Guo Dao’s complicated feelings.

He sighed softly. “I know who you like. But you also know that anyone in this world may pursue her—except you.”

Guo Dao’s expression remained calm, but his fist clenched tightly within his sleeve. “You do not need to remind me. I understand perfectly well. I will not let Mother notice my thoughts, nor will I let anyone else discover them. Don’t worry.”

With those words, he turned and walked away quickly, disappearing from sight.

Guo Cheng watched him leave, his heart growing even heavier. Guo Dao was his beloved younger brother, and he did not want this matter to damage the harmony of their family.

Just then, a soft voice sounded behind him.

“Third Brother, what are you doing here?”

Startled, Guo Cheng turned around and saw Li Weiyang smiling at him. He opened his mouth, but for a moment, he did not know what to say.

Li Weiyang asked softly, “Is Third Brother worried that Fifth Brother will not win this time?”

Guo Cheng was stunned for a moment, then nodded. “Yes. I am worried about him.”

Before he could continue, Li Weiyang seemed to understand something. She sighed softly. “If you are not worried about him losing, then you must be worried that he does not like Princess Shouchun.”

Her words struck directly at the heart of the matter, causing Guo Cheng’s expression to change.

For a moment, he wondered whether Li Weiyang had already noticed Guo Dao’s feelings for her. Given her intelligence, it was certainly possible. But he quickly dismissed the thought. Li Weiyang was too clever not to understand that, regardless of Guo Dao’s feelings, the two of them could never be together. “their relationship had long since been decided—they could only ever remain siblings in name.

Guo Cheng could only give up on the matter. Perhaps after meeting the beautiful Princess Shouchun, Guo Dao might change his mind.

…

Ten days later, Princess Shouchun’s husband selection would officially begin, followed by civil and martial examinations. Any unmarried man under the age of thirty who was the son of an official of the fourth rank or above in Yuexi, or who belonged to the nobility, was eligible to participate in the literary examination. The top ten candidates would then proceed to the martial examination, and the victor would become Princess Shouchun’s husband.

Naturally, the selection of a fuma was an extremely meticulous affair.

[^1]: Fuma — the title given to a princess’s consort; literally, the emperor’s son-in-law.

Although Princess Shouchun was not Empress Pei’s daughter, she was gentle, beautiful, and skilled at pleasing the Emperor. As a result, Empress Pei treated her with exceptional care and affection, in sharp contrast to Princess Daming, who had long fallen out of favor.

The officials of the Ministry of Rites were busy preparing for the selection. They carefully screened the candidates from the aristocratic families, examining their appearance, literary talent, and martial abilities. Every stage was conducted rigorously, and anyone who failed to meet the standards was eliminated immediately.

In the end, only fifty young men from the entire capital were permitted to take the literary examination, and only the top ten would advance to the martial examination. Because the process was so rigorous, the imperial son-in-law selection naturally became the grandest event of the year in Yuexi.

Today was the official day of the selection.

When Li Weiyang arrived at Jinhua Gate, the spacious grounds before the gate were already filled with viewing platforms and temporary tents. The ceremony was about to begin. High above sat the Emperor, the Empress, the imperial consorts, and the beautiful Princess Shouchun. Nearby, the noble families occupied separate tents prepared for them. Even many lesser families had come to watch the excitement, turning the once-empty grounds before Jinhua Gate into a bustling sea of voices and movement.
