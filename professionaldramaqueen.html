Professional Drama Queen

Chinese Name: 庶女有毒

Released: 2012

Author: Qin Jian

Synopsis

As a True Drama Queen, Yuan Su's Goal Is to Torment the Villains Until They Kneel Before Her and Call Her Daddy!

After all, **daddy is still daddy!**¹

【The Real and Fake Divas】²

Twin Sister:
“You have to be my substitute. Sing in my place, appear on reality shows in my place, and become my shadow.”

Female Protagonist:
“Sure, sure! But once I become famous, who knows which one of us will be the substitute for the other?”

---

【The Villainous Stepmother's Descent into Darkness】³

Stepson & Stepdaughter:
“Even though you've treated us well, our biological mother is still our mother. Once you've raised us to adulthood, you can get lost.”

Female Protagonist:
“Dump the baggage, collect the support money, and live the good life! What could be more carefree?”⁴

---

Author's Notes

1. It's a marry su novel And our heroine has a golden finger!

2. The male lead is the same person throughout the entire novel. In every world, the heroine will live out an entire lifetime with him, right up until his death.

3. If this isn't your cup of tea, please click the × and move along!

📌 Tiny terminology notes

¹ “Daddy is still daddy” (你爸爸还是你爸爸) — Chinese internet slang meaning “I'm still the one on top, no matter how things change.”

² 天后 (tiānhòu) — Literally “Queen of Heaven,” but in entertainment slang it means a hugely successful female singer/diva.

³ 黑化 (hēihuà) — Literally “turn black”; webnovel slang for someone becoming ruthless, villainous, or morally darker.

⁴ 拖油瓶 (tuōyóupíng) — Literally “an oil bottle being dragged along”; an old expression for unwanted baggage, especially stepchildren brought into a marriage.

⁵ 金手指 (jīnshǒuzhǐ) — Literally “golden finger”; a protagonist's cheat ability, such as a system, supernatural power, special knowledge, or other unfair advantage.

Translator verdict:
“Don't come here looking for angst and realism. Our heroine is going into every world, stealing the spotlight, humiliating villains, getting her revenge, and living an outrageously satisfying life. The same ML follows her through every world, and they'll spend an entire lifetime together each time.”
