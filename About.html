✨About Me

Hi! I'm the person behind BobaTeaSparkle. 💕

☕ A little about me

I'm a reader, a translator, and—perhaps unfortunately for my sleep schedule—a person who gets far too attached to fictional characters. 😂

I started BobaTeaSparkle because I wanted to share some of the Chinese webnovels I've enjoyed with more readers.

📖 The kind of stories I love

I especially love stories with clever female protagonists, complicated characters, satisfying revenge, historical intrigue, transmigration, and the occasional completely unhinged plot twist.

Basically, if a heroine is about to turn someone's carefully planned scheme upside down, I'm probably already invested. 😂

✍️ Why I translate

I translate because I genuinely enjoy these stories, and I want my translations to feel just as enjoyable to read as the originals.

I try to keep the author's atmosphere, characters, humor, and intentions while making the English flow naturally.

🔎 One small warning...

You'll occasionally find my little notes and footnotes scattered around the chapters—because apparently I cannot read something interesting without stopping to research it and overexplaining evrything . 😭

🧋 About BobaTeaSparkle

BobaTeaSparkle is still a small project, and I'm building it one chapter at a time.

There are only a couple of stories here for now, but I have a lot more planned.

So grab your tea, pick a story, and welcome to my little corner of the internet.

— The person behind BobaTeaSparkle

P.S. If you see me getting emotionally attached to a villainess, no you didn't. 😂
